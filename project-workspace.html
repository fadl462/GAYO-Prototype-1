Theory of Change & Logframe

If households are equipped with segregation infrastructure and sustained behaviour-change messaging, and if recycling markets provide reliable income for waste-pickers, then municipal waste diversion will increase and landfill dependency will fall across the five target sub-districts.

Lessons Learned & Recommendations
Success
Pairing recycling stations with a vendor income scheme drove faster household enrollment than sensitization alone.
Challenge
Council permit delays in Sub-district 5 pushed back two station installations by six weeks.
Recommendation
Initiate permit applications at proposal stage for future municipal waste projects.
