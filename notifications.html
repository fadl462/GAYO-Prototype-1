Notification & Escalation Rules
Who gets notified, and when it escalates
Key deadline reminders
Sent to the officer responsible and their line manager in the same alert, so deadlines are never visible to only one person.
Overdue activity escalation
If an activity remains overdue for more than 48 hours, the responsible officer's line manager is automatically added to the alert.
Budget threshold alerts
Notifies the project lead and the Finance Manager together when utilization crosses 95% or falls critically behind schedule.
MERL verification requests
Routed to the data owner and the MERL Lead until the flagged indicator is verified or resolved.
